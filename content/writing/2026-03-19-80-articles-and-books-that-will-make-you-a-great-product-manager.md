# 80 Articles and Books that will Make you a Great Product Manager

Source: [medium.com](https://medium.com/@noah_weiss/50-articles-and-books-that-will-make-you-a-great-product-manager-aad5babee2f7)

## TL;DR
The article "80 Articles and Books that will Make you a Great Product Manager" by Noah Weiss provides a comprehensive list of resources for product managers, including articles, books, and guides on various topics such as product leadership, design, strategy, and team management.

## Key Ideas
The article highlights the importance of continuous learning and self-improvement for product managers, and provides a curated list of resources to help them develop their skills and knowledge. The resources cover a wide range of topics, including product management fundamentals, leadership, design, and strategy.

## What I Agree With
I agree with the author's emphasis on the importance of continuous learning and self-improvement for product managers. The list of resources provided is comprehensive and covers a wide range of topics, making it a valuable resource for product managers at all levels.

## What I Challenge
I challenge the assumption that reading 80 articles and books is necessary to become a great product manager. While the resources listed are valuable, it's possible to become a great product manager through experience, mentorship, and practice, in addition to reading and learning from others.

## Actionable Takeaways
Some actionable takeaways from the article include:
* Start with the "must-read" articles and books listed, such as "Good Product Manager/Bad Product Manager" and "The Innovator's Dilemma"
* Focus on developing a strong understanding of product management fundamentals, including design, strategy, and leadership
* Prioritize continuous learning and self-improvement, and seek out mentorship and feedback from experienced product managers.
